Hackathon project

Can RSI improve an LLM's understanding of physics?

The problem
AI-generated scenes look real, but their motion often breaks the physics.

Newton's laws. Energy conservation. Momentum.

Our idea

Put an agent in the improvement loop.

Model draws
the scene
→
Agent finds
failures
→
Agent writes data,
verifiable reward
→
Train a small Qwen
on that reward
Examples

A correct bounce, and a failure we catch.

Results

Success rate on cases it never saw.

Held-out scenes, before → after training
In-distribution scenes
Takeaway
Recursive self-improvement, for physics.
