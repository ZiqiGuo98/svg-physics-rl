Can RSI improve an LLM's understanding of physics?

A stronger model improving a weaker one, tested on a ball and a wall.

Background

Generated scenes now look strikingly real.

Precise objects from a prompt

The problem

But the motion often breaks the physics.

Newton's laws

Speed changes for no reason.

Energy conservation

A bounce returns more energy than went in.

Contact

Does the ball come back off the wall, or pass through?

Long videos

Errors accumulate, so physics can drift over time.
